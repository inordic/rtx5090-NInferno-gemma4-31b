## 2. Patch 19

The last layer is a full-attention layer no later layer reads through. On a mid-prompt call only its keys matter, and those are written before its attention starts, so the patch stops there. A rebuild, a causal score and a call ending on a capture's frontier keep the full pass ([research update §2](2026-11-04-research-update.md#2-prefill)). It is opt-in: `NINFERNO_GEMMA4_SKIP_LAST_LAYER=1`.

Measured on `apps-up4` at production flags, off against on:

| | Off | On | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2K | 4,156 | 4,212 | +1.3% |
| 8K | 4,451 | 4,525 | +1.7% |
| 32K | 3,444 | 3,582 | +4.0% |
| 64K | 2,565 | 2,710 | +5.7% |
| 130K | 1,682 | 1,809 | +7.6% |

We had estimated +2.6%, +4.6% and +8% at 8K, 32K and 130K.

[`n5ab.py`](../bench/runs/night5/n5ab.py) sent greedy requests at 48K, 64K and 100K: cold, repeated, and as a follow-up turn. All 30 replies were token-identical off against off, and off against on.

## 4. Prefill chunk

W1 first tried chunk 1280 on production's build: +3.3% at 8K, +3.1% at 32K, but 3,456 tokens of context and only 8.6 MiB left. Chunk 1792 did not fit at any context down to 180,224.

The assistant in NVFP4 frees 0.22 GiB, which changed that. F1 swept chunks with patch 19 and the new assistant, each at the largest context it fit (three repeats, median):

| Chunk | Context | Free MiB | 2K | 8K | 32K | 64K |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1024 | 206,848 | 274.6 | 4,172 | 4,513 | 3,522 | 2,691 |
| 1152 | 206,848 | 172.6 | 4,030 | 4,447 | 3,453 | 2,627 |
| **1280** | 206,848 | 70.6 | **4,209** | **4,765** | **3,672** | **2,771** |
| 1408 | 204,800 | 8.6 | 3,968 | 4,475 | 3,466 | 2,647 |
| 1536 | 198,656 | 26.6 | 4,131 | 4,662 | 3,574 | 2,702 |

Odd multiples of 128 lose to chunk 1024. Chunk 1280 wins at every length.

We chose chunk 1280, not the larger context chunk 1024 could have:

- The live traffic puts 47% of its new prompt tokens in requests of 16K–64K new tokens, 21% at 4K–16K and 21% above 64K, so prefill at those sizes is what counts. Only 2.4% come from requests of 1K or less, which the chunk does not touch.
- The agent's largest prompt so far is 138K.

Chunk 1024 fits 217,088 with 72.6 MiB left, so about 213K at production's margin. That stays open if context ever matters more than 5% of prefill.

**Context.** At 206,848 and chunk 1280 the plan drops to `reserve warm_up` with 70.6 MiB left. At 203,136 it reports `reserve kept` and 144.6 MiB, exactly production's margin, so that is what runs. Over F2's whole battery, free memory fell by 14 MiB.

Chunk 1280 pins more host memory for the 18 host states: 16.1 GiB against 14.3 GiB. The container has room: 25 GB used of 48.

## 7. What did not pay

**Skip-softmax.** [`n5skip.py`](../bench/runs/night5/n5skip.py) counted, on the 512-wide layers, the score tiles whose largest value falls below a threshold:

| Prompt | 2^-6 | 2^-8 | 2^-10 | 2^-12 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8K | 8.4% | 1.6% | 0.3% | 0.1% |
| 32K | 14.2% | 3.5% | 0.6% | 0.1% |
| 64K | 16.1% | 4.0% | 0.7% | 0.1% |
| 131K | 17.7% | 4.4% | 0.7% | 0.1% |

The bar was 25% at 2^-10. A second pass on 32K and 131K prompts gave the same picture. The idea stops here: this model's full-attention layers do not leave enough empty tiles.

**P0 profile.** The card was at P0 with and without it, and the speed row sits within noise of the same build in W7.

**CUDA 13.4.** W8 stopped on `nvtx3/nvToolsExt.h`; `cuda-minimal-build-13-4` doesn't carry it. With `cuda-nvtx-13-4` added the tree built. Tokens were identical (30/30), and speed was the same or slightly lower: 8K −0.3%, 32K −0.8%, 64K −1.3%, decode the same. The install had moved `/usr/local/cuda` to 13.4 through alternatives; we set it back to 13.2.

**KLD.** `ninferno-perplexity` scored against a run with exact activations and BF16 KV (mean KLD, median, share of positions with the same top token):

| | Mean | Median | Top-1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 4-bit activations and FP8 KV (production) | 1.285 | 0.195 | 68.2% |
| FP8 KV alone | 0.350 | 0.008 | 87.6% |
| 4-bit activations alone | 1.242 | 0.190 | 68.6% |

Only the order is usable: nearly all of the difference comes from the 4-bit activations, little from FP8 KV. The absolute numbers are not a quality score. The reference's own perplexity is 4,234 on this corpus (19,696 on Wikipedia text, 48 on source code), far from what a working language model gives, and production comes out 15% "better" than the reference. An instruction-tuned model on raw text is the likely cause, but we did not confirm it. The tool needs chat-formatted text before its numbers can stand beside the question suites.

**The false alarm.** The stress run's cached share came out lower than W3's: 78.8% against 83.4%, with 27% more prefill work. Cache reuse is worth more than any prefill gain, so the deploy waited for a test. F4 ran the same stress on a fresh server, chunk 1280 against 1024, two seeds:

| Seed | Chunk 1280 | Chunk 1024 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | 92.5% | 92.3% |
| 6 | 90.7% | 91.5% |

There is no chunk effect. The stress deliberately overflows the cache, and which session gets evicted shifts with small differences; both gate runs also started on a cache filled by the replays, hence the lower numbers. The replays, closer to a real day, gave the same cached share on both setups.
